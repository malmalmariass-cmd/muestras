01

Sign in

Service Report → Technician
  1. Tap Technician.
  2. Type your Employee ID (for example MS26).
  3. Enter your PIN with the keypad. C clears it and ⌫ deletes one digit.
  4. Tap Sign in.
Tip: check Tutorial mode to get an explanation of each section while you use the app. Choose English or Español at the bottom.
Your ID and PIN are created by your supervisor. Ask them if you forget your PIN.
02

My jobs

Jobs

Jobs shows the visits the office assigned to you, grouped by day. Tap Refresh to load new jobs.

Job details

Tap a job to see the instructions from the office, the appointment, the address with a Map link and the customer's contact. Tap Start report or Equipment service to open the report with the customer already filled in.

Postpone or reschedule

  • Postpone (same day) — choose a new time today and tap Confirm.
  • Reschedule — choose a new date and time and tap Confirm.

WhatsApp reminder

Tap WhatsApp reminder to remind the customer about the visit.

Ask to cancel

If the customer cancels, tap Ask to cancel, write why it should be cancelled and tap Confirm. Your supervisor approves the cancellation.

03

Field service report

Field Service Report — repairs and fixes

Customer and job

  1. Open Field Service Report (or Start report from a job).
  2. Choose the Customer name from the list. A report number is reserved for you automatically.
  3. Check the address, WhatsApp and email. Your Employee ID is filled in for you.
  4. Write any Instructions or notes for the job.
Registered customers: only the supervisor can change their data. If something changed, update it in the report — your change goes with the report so the supervisor can approve it.

Related serial numbers

Enter the machine's Model, Serial No. and Make, plus the Hour meter reading, Location and Delivery date.

Daily time record

For each day, enter the Date, the time you Left the shop, Started and Completed the job, and the Mileage. Tap + Add day for jobs that take more than one day; tap ✕ to remove a day.

Faults

Describe each problem in Fault 1, Fault 2… (tap + Add). Tap + Add correction to explain how you fixed it.

Parts & materials

  1. Tap + Request parts.
  2. Choose In stock (shop) or Order from vendor.
  3. Search the part, set the quantity with – / + and tap Add. Use + Other shop material for items not in inventory.
  4. Add an optional note for the supervisor and tap Send to supervisor.
What happens next: the request appears in the report as Waiting for supervisor. The supervisor prepares the quote; you show it to the customer.

Status and customer comment

Tap the current status: Job in progress Awaiting parts Follow up repair Return to client Complete. Each change is saved in the Status history. The customer can write a comment before signing. Tap Schedule follow-up check if the machine needs another visit.

Signatures and sending

  1. Tap a signature box: Serviceman, Supervisor or Customer.
  2. Sign with your finger or a stylus. Tap Clear to try again, then Accept signature.
  3. Tap Send / PDF. The report goes to the office and the invoice number is assigned.
Tip: the office copy is emailed automatically when the customer signs. Tap Save to keep working later, or New to start a blank report.
04

History

History → My reports
  • Search by invoice, customer, serial or model.
  • Open to continue a report, PDF to view it, Copy to start a new report with the same customer and machine.
  • Tags show whether a report is signed and in office.
  • Sync now sends your reports right away; Download my reports from the office brings them back to this tablet.
Offline friendly: reports are saved on the tablet and sent to the office automatically when there is internet. Invoice numbers are assigned by the office on sync.
05

Equipment service report

Equipment Service Report — maintenance

Use this report for maintenance visits. Fill in the customer, the machine and the time record as in the field service report.

Services performed

Check every service you did — for example Change engine oil, Change hydraulic filter, Grease all points, Check tires / tracks. Add a note under each one if needed, and tap + Other service for anything not on the list.

Schedule a follow-up check

  1. Tap Schedule follow-up check.
  2. Choose the Date and Time and write What to check.
  3. Check The customer agrees to a WhatsApp reminder if they agree.
  4. Tap Schedule visit. The visit appears in Jobs, highlighted the day before and on the day.

Sign and save

Collect the signatures and tap Save or Send / PDF.

06

Parts requests

Parts Requests → My parts requests

Every parts request you sent appears here with its status (for example Waiting for supervisor), the customer and the machine.

  • Edit to change a request, or Ask to cancel.
  • + New request to ask for parts without a report.
  • Vendors to see the supplier list, Refresh to see the latest answers.
