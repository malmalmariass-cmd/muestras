Troubleshooting

Don't see the option?

Using another browser on iPhone?Chrome and Edge also work: tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Older iOS versions need Safari.
In a private or Incognito tab?Switch to a normal tab. Chrome on Android hides the option in Incognito.
Samsung Internet?Tap ≡ → Add page to → Home screen.
Firefox on Android?Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
Still missing?Update your browser and your phone's software, then try again.
Want to remove it?Long-press the icon and choose Remove App or Uninstall.
