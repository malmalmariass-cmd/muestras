The red numbers next to a menu item show how many things are waiting for you. Click Tips at any time for help. A narrated video version of this manual is also available: Supervisor_Panel_Tutorial.mp4.

01

Service reports

Reports & Jobs → Service reports

Every report sent from the technicians' tablets appears here, newest first.

  • The yellow box warns about empty reports (nothing written, no signatures) and possible duplicates (same customer, day, type and machine). Choose Open, Keep or Delete.
  • Filter by Search, Technician, Status, Type and dates. Click Export CSV to download the list.

Open a report

Click a report number. The panel shows the customer and job, equipment, daily time record, services performed, parts requests, job status and signatures.

Tip: click View full history of S/N… to see all the work ever done on that machine.

Approve a customer data change

If a technician asks to change the customer's data (for example a new email), a yellow box appears at the top of the report. Review it and click Approve and update the customer.

Sign as supervisor

  1. Scroll down to Signatures and click Sign as supervisor.
  2. Sign with the mouse, a finger or a pen. Click Clear to start over.
  3. Click Accept signature. The message "Signed — the job is complete" confirms it.

Delete a report

Open the report and click Delete. Write the reason and enter your supervisor PIN — both are required.

Good to know: in-house parts go back to stock, and a copy of the deleted report stays in Settings → Activity log.
02

Employees

Employees → + Add employee
  1. Type the Full name.
  2. Enter an Employee ID (for example MS26). It is used to sign in on the tablet.
  3. Choose the Role: Technician or Supervisor.
  4. Set a PIN of 4 to 8 digits.
  5. Leave Active (can sign in) checked and click Save.
Tip: to remove someone's access, click Delete on their row, or uncheck Active.
03

Schedule a job

Reports & Jobs → Jobs & appointments → + New job
  1. Customer: pick a saved customer or type a new name.
  2. Technician: choose who will do the visit.
  3. Check Customer agrees to WhatsApp reminders if the customer agreed.
  4. Set the Appointment date and time.
  5. Repeat: for regular maintenance choose a frequency, e.g. Every week.
  6. Fill in the Address, Customer WhatsApp and email.
  7. Optional: Make, Model, Serial No., Work order #, Location and Instructions for the technician.
  8. Click Save job.
Recurring jobs: the next visit is created automatically as soon as the customer and the supervisor sign the report. Overdue jobs are marked in orange.
04

Manage jobs

Reports & Jobs → Jobs & appointments

Cancel requests from technicians

When a technician asks to cancel a job, the job is highlighted in the list. Open it and, at the bottom, click Approve — cancel the job or Decline.

Reschedule, reassign or delete

  • Move to date / Time → Reschedule to move the visit.
  • WhatsApp reminder, Cancel job and Follow-up visit done are at the bottom of the job.
  • Change the Technician to give the job to someone else, then click Save job.
  • A cancelled job can be Reopened. Click Delete to remove a job completely.
05

Create an invoice

Invoices → + New invoice

Customer

  • The customer pays — a person or a company pays directly.
  • A person pays for a company we already serve — the invoice goes in the company's name. Pick the company and write the Contact name; the invoice says Attn: this person.
  • Address, phone and email fill in automatically. Keep Update this customer's information in Customers checked to save changes.

Work order and lines

  • Work order (service report) is required. Choose the service report from the list, or Sale without a service report (parts / counter sale).
  • + Labor / service — enter hours (Qty) and rate (Unit price).
  • + In-house part — search the part and click Add. In-house parts come out of the inventory automatically.
  • + Part from a vendor — vendor parts go to Orders.
  • Check Tax on taxable lines. Subtotal, sales tax and total are calculated for you.
  • Optional Notes for the customer, then click Save invoice.
Red messages: if something is missing, a red message above the buttons tells you what to fix — for example the contact name or the work order.

Unsaved invoices

If you leave an invoice without saving, a yellow banner appears. Click Open to finish it or Delete to discard it.

Remember: unsaved drafts are deleted automatically after 24 hours.
06

Parts & Orders

Parts & Orders → Parts requests · Orders to vendors · Vendors

Parts requests shows the parts your technicians ask for. The cards at the top count requests waiting for you, quoted, approved and ready to invoice.

Send a quote

  1. Click a request to open it (technician, customer, machine and parts).
  2. Check the Unit price and change it if needed. You can add a line or choose a vendor.
  3. Write a Message to the technician, e.g. "available now, full payment required".
  4. Click Send quote to technician. The technician shows it to the customer.

Approve and reserve

When the customer accepts, click Customer said yes — approve & reserve parts. In-house parts are reserved for 2 hours; use +2 h +8 h +1 day to extend.

Create the invoice

  1. Click Create invoice. Customer, parts and prices are filled in, and the Reference shows the parts request number.
  2. In Work order, choose the service report these parts belong to, or Sale without a service report if they were sold on their own.
  3. Click Save invoice.

Orders to vendors and Vendors

Orders to vendors lists the parts you need to buy from a supplier. To save a supplier, open Vendors and click + Add vendor: enter the company, contact person, phone, WhatsApp, email, website, your account number with them, address and notes, then click Save vendor.

Stock: parts leave the stock only when the invoice is made.
07

Manage invoices

Invoices → click an invoice

Unsaved changes

If you leave changes to an invoice without saving, a yellow banner appears at the top of every page. Click Open to go back to it, or Delete to discard the changes.

Edit an invoice

  1. Open the invoice and click Edit.
  2. In Work order, open the list and choose the service report this invoice belongs to. Each report shows its number, date and machine.
  3. Click Save invoice.

Record a payment

Under Payments, enter the Amount, Method, Date and a Note (check number, reference…), then click + Record payment. The Balance due updates automatically.

Important: payments are not collected in the app. Here you only record what the customer paid (cash, check, Zelle…).

Change the status

Under Status, choose a new status (for example Paid), add an optional note and click Update status. Every change is saved in the Status history with the date and the person who made it.

Void or delete

  • Void invoice cancels the invoice and returns its in-house parts to stock. The invoice stays in the list marked as Void.
  • Delete removes the invoice completely. Write the reason and enter your supervisor PIN. A copy stays in Settings → Activity log.
08

Customers

Customers

The list shows each customer's contacts, number of reports and last service. Customers typed on the tablets are added automatically.

Import a list

Click Import Excel / Word to load customers from Excel, CSV, Word, or a photo of a table. Click Download template to get the format. Duplicates are merged and existing customers are never overwritten.

Add or edit a customer

  • + Add customer creates a customer by hand. Add extra contacts (reports and invoices can be sent to them) and check Frequent customer for regulars.
  • To edit, search the name and click it. Update the details and click Save.

Schedule or bill from the customer card

In the customer card, click Schedule job to open a new job with the customer's details already filled in, or New invoice to bill them right away.

09

Inventory & Equipment

Inventory · Equipment

Inventory shows every part with its price, quantity in stock, status and the total stock value. Use + Add part or Import Excel / Word, and Export CSV to download.

Equipment lists every machine serviced, grouped by serial number. Open one to see all the work done on it.

10

Statistics

Statistics

Choose a period — This month Last 30 days Last 90 days This year All time — and, if you want, a Technician.

You will see service reports, hours worked, signed and completed reports, customers served, parts used, reports per week, top customers, most serviced makes and top parts.

11

Settings

Settings
  • Next invoice number — numbers are assigned automatically, in order, so two tablets never get the same number.
  • Invoices — default Sales tax (%) and Labor rate per hour (USD) for every new invoice.
  • Security & backup — Download full backup (.sql) or Export all data (Excel), and Change my PIN.
  • Activity log — who did what, and when, including deleted reports and invoices.
Best practice: download a full backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe.
